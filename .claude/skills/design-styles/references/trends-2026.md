## What is in the air
- **Calm vs. loud split.** One side: quiet luxury, serif revival, Cloud Dancer off-whites, generous space
  (G2/G3). The other: dopamine color, maximal type, collage, "imperfect by design" (G9/G15/G16). Audience decides.
- **Imperfect by design.** Hand-made marks, grain, slight irregularity as a counter to AI-perfect imagery
  (Canva's 2026 report names the year this way). Use it as a modifier on clean structure.
- **Serif revival + warm grounds.** Notably AI and knowledge brands pair serifs with paper-like backgrounds to
  signal warmth and trust.
- **Type as the image.** Oversized, extended, condensed or distorted display type carries the composition.
- **Tactile depth returns.** Soft 3D, glass (Apple's Liquid Glass) and light skeuomorphism, kept simple
  enough to stay legible.
- **Neo-brutalism and retro web** remain strong for developer and youth brands.
- **Color:** Pantone 2026 = Cloud Dancer (a white). Earthy and organic branding palettes; teal and green in forecasts;
  saturated dopamine color in social.

## Using trends well
- Trends inform the **execution** (type, texture, palette temperature), not the concept.
- Long-lived assets (logos, icon systems) take at most one trend accent. Campaigns can go all-in.
- Every trend choice still passes the contrast checks (style_board.py) and the target-platform limits.
