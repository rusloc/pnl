## 2. Period, comparison and filters

- A-10 **Period presets:** 7 d · **30 d (default)** · MTD · QTD · Custom (from / to on `dim_date`, actual days only). A window is a set of whole calendar days ending at or before the as-of day. Granularity for trend charts: **Day (default)** · Week (Mon–Sun, `dim_date.week_start`) · Month.
- A-11 **Comparison modes:** **Previous period (default)** = the same number of days immediately before the window · Same period last year = the same calendar dates one year back (`dim_date.ly_date`) · Plan = the window's plan (Spend) or target (customers, CAC, payback, LTV:CAC; n/a on New MRR) · None. Every vital Δ and every "vs prior" card follows the mode; the healthy-range verdicts (A-24) never do.
- A-12 **Window filters** (the Filters window): Platform (Web / iOS / Android, multi), Market (All / Global / NA / EMEA, campaign attribute) [OQ 10], Tier (All / Self-serve / Sales-assisted), Channel › Network (tree, multi), Campaign (searchable multi-select) with Status facets (Live / Learning / Paused / Ended; **default Live + Learning**), Attribution model (single: **Data-driven (default)** · Last click · First touch · Platform-reported). Platform, Market, Tier, Channel, Network and Status filter through `dim_campaign` (A-71): every fact of a filtered-out campaign disappears.
- A-13 **Precedence:** window filters ∩ cross-filter ∩ the view's own scope. A **cross-filter** is a click on a channel row (C1) or a campaign row (P1) and scopes every body card of the current and following views; **the vitals ribbon never re-scopes** (it follows the window filters only). Clearing the cross-filter clears it everywhere.
- A-14 **Chips line:** one text segment per active non-default filter plus `Selected: <name>` for a cross-filter; " - " as the separator (parity rule 6); hidden when nothing is active. Power BI: a measure-built line, Reset = *Clear all slicers*; JS: removable chips, dashed outline for the cross-filter chip.
- A-15 **Saved views** (five, read-only): Exec weekly (Overview · 30 d · all) · Daily pacing (Campaigns · MTD vs Plan) · Mobile only (Channels · iOS + Android) · Brand vs non-brand (Campaigns · paid search) · Finance month-end (Cohorts · Last click). Power BI: bookmarks with data state; JS: preset filter states.
- A-16 **Attribution model switch** applies to paid customers and New MRR (and so to CAC, CPA, ROAS, payback, LTV:CAC, the funnel's Paid stage and the top movers); sign-ups, activations, trials, cohorts and retention do not switch (A-73). The model name is in the footer and in every ⓘ of an affected card.
- A-17 **Freshness** [OQ 11]: per source in `sync_log`, age = refresh stamp − `last_sync_ts`; the dot is green when every source's age < its `stale_after_h`, amber when any source's age ≥ `stale_after_h` (6 h hourly, 30 h daily), red when any ≥ `red_after_h` (24 h / 72 h). Header text "Synced n h ago" = age of the oldest hourly source (**2 h**). The footer lists each source's last sync.

## 4. Metric definitions (binding for the generator and every flavour)

"Window" = the selected days; "scope" = the window filters ∩ cross-filter; amounts USD; full precision in measures, rounding only at display (A-49). Values in brackets are the default-window ALL anchors.

- A-30 **Spend** = Σ `fact_ad_delivery_day.spend_usd`. Agency fee and team cost are not in spend [OQ 4]. [$188.4k; plan $176.0k; prev $176.1k]
- A-31 **Plan** = Σ over the window's days of `fact_budget_month.budget_usd ÷ days_in_month` for the scope's campaigns. **Pacing %** = Spend ÷ Plan. [plan 176.0k; pacing 107 %; channels 106 / 119 / 101 / 108 / 84 / 80 %]
- A-32 **Impressions, Clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM** from `fact_ad_delivery_day`: CTR = clicks ÷ impressions; CPC = spend ÷ clicks; CPM = spend ÷ impressions × 1000. [14.2 M; 223.0k; 1.57 %; $0.84; $13.3]
- A-33 **Sessions (web)** = Σ `fact_traffic_day.sessions`; **Store views, Installs, First opens (mobile)** = Σ the store-page columns; **Landed** = sessions + installs; landing rate web = sessions ÷ clicks (web campaigns); store CVR = installs ÷ store views; install → sign-up = sign-ups ÷ installs (mobile campaigns). [114.8k; 21.7k; store → install **29.4 %** all-mobile (App Store 31 %, Google Play 27 % binding); install → sign-up 15.8 %; web land **79.7 %** *(Amended 2026-11-04 after phase 1: was 31 % / 75 % — D-3)*]
- A-34 **Sign-ups** = Σ `fact_funnel_day.signups` (= Σ `fact_traffic_day.signups`, AT-05); **CPL** = spend ÷ sign-ups; click → sign-up = sign-ups ÷ clicks. [6,980; $27; 3.1 %]
- A-35 **Activated** = Σ `step_first_dashboard` (first dashboard created within 7 days of sign-up, cohorted by sign-up day [OQ 6]); activation rate = ÷ sign-ups; cost per activated = spend ÷ activated. The last 7 days of any window are partial by construction (ⓘ note). [2,860; 41 %; $66]
- A-36 **Trials** = Σ `trials`; trial rate = trials ÷ sign-ups; **Trial → paid** = Paid customers ÷ trials. [3,220; 46 %; 14.2 %, prev 16.1 %]
- A-37 **Paid customers** = Σ `fact_attribution_day.paid_customers` for the selected model (DDA default); fractional at campaign and channel scope, integer at ALL for the first-party models. **CPA** = spend ÷ paid customers. [457; $412]
- A-38 **Paid CAC** = Spend ÷ Paid customers (= CPA at the same scope). **Team cost** of a window = Σ days × `PAID_TEAM_COST_MONTH_USD` (valid row) ÷ days_in_month; **allocated to a scope pro rata to its spend share of the window**. **Blended CAC** = (Spend + allocated team cost) ÷ Paid customers. [$412; team $54.1k *(Amended 2026-11-04 after phase 1: was $54.3k; `PAID_TEAM_COST_MONTH_USD` steps to 55,730 on 2026-10-05, not 54,300 on 2026-10-01 — D-2)*; $531]
- A-39 **New MRR** = Σ `fact_attribution_day.new_mrr_usd` (selected model); **ARPA** = New MRR ÷ Paid customers. [$61.9k; $135]
- A-40 **CAC payback (months)** = Blended CAC ÷ (ARPA × `GROSS_MARGIN_PCT`) [OQ 3]. [10.8; prev 10.2]
- A-41 **Monthly logo churn** = Σ `churned` ÷ Σ `customers_start` over `fact_cohort_revenue_month` rows whose calendar month (cohort month + offset) falls in the trailing 3 complete months of the window's end, offsets ≥ 1, scope's campaigns. **LTV** = ARPA × `GROSS_MARGIN_PCT` ÷ churn; **LTV:CAC** = LTV ÷ Blended CAC. [2.72 %; 3.4×]
- A-42 **ROAS (90-day)** = New MRR × R90 ÷ Spend, where **R90** = Σ `revenue_usd` offsets 0–2 ÷ `revenue_usd` offset 0 of the newest cohort month with three complete offset months, at the scope (campaign → channel → ALL fallback). [search 1.31 · social 0.74 · app 0.98 · display 0.62 · retargeting 1.90 · affiliates 1.22]
- A-43 **Platform-reported vs modelled:** reported CPA = spend ÷ `paid_customers` of model PLATFORM; **attribution gap** = PLATFORM paid customers ÷ modelled (selected first-party model) − 1. [+38 %; social +72 %, display +95 %]
- A-44 **Frequency (7-day)** = Σ impressions of the window's last 7 days ÷ Σ `reach_7d` on the window's last day, at creative / ad set / campaign scope. **CTR 7-day slope** = CTR of the last 7 days ÷ CTR of the 7 days before − 1. [`SOC_META_LAL2`: 3.4, −46 %; creative `Dashboard in 60s · v3`: 3.4, −46 %, CTR 1.82 %] A campaign's frequency cannot exceed its highest creative's; `SOC_META_LAL2` equals its top creative because that creative carries ≈ 97.5 % of the last 14 days' impressions *(Amended 2026-11-04 after phase 1, D-5)*.
- A-45 **Terms:** CPC = spend ÷ clicks, CVR = `signups` ÷ clicks, Conv. = `signups`, from `fact_term_day`.
- A-46 **Landing pages:** sessions, CVR = sign-ups ÷ sessions (web) or installs ÷ store views (store pages), bounce = bounces ÷ sessions, LCP = sessions-weighted mean of `lcp_ms_p75` (web only). [App Store 31 % (prev 31 %), Google Play 27 % (prev 28 %)]
- A-47 **Onboarding steps** = Σ of each `step_*` column ÷ Σ sign-ups, in `dim_onboarding_step.sort_order`. **Sign-up → activation time** = the seven `act_*` bins as shares of activated within 30 days; **median hours** by linear interpolation inside the median bin (bin edges 0 / 1 / 6 / 24 / 48 / 96 / 168 / 720 h). **Activation by segment** = `activated_7d` ÷ `signups` per `dim_platform_segment`. [100 → 88 → 81 → 52 → 41 → 23 %; 26 h — window bin shares 8.6 / 20.2 / 19.3 / 22.2 / 13.3 / 8.8 / 7.5 %, right-censored (the fully observed previous period reads 35 h) *(Amended 2026-11-04 after phase 1: the mockup's 14 / 19 / 17 / 16 / 12 / 9 / 13 % give ≈ 24 h — D-10)*; 46 / 38 / 47 / 44 / 39 / 29 %]
- A-48 **Cohorts** (default model, scope's campaigns, acquisition months per A-56; `customers_start` / `churned` / `customers_end` / `refund_count` are DDA credits, fractional per campaign — *(Amended 2026-11-04 after phase 1, D-7)*; the partial end month has offset 0 only — D-17): **payback curve** value at offset m = Σ_{k ≤ m} (`revenue_usd` − `refunds_usd`) × `GROSS_MARGIN_PCT` ÷ `customers_start`(offset 0) ÷ cohort blended CAC, where cohort blended CAC = (spend of the cohort month + allocated team cost) ÷ `customers_start`(offset 0); **paid back** at the first m ≥ 100 %; **retention D-N** = `users_active` ÷ `users_matured` from `fact_retention_cohort_month` (cell shown only where written, A-74); **refund rate** = Σ `refund_count` ÷ Σ `customers_start`(offset 0) over the 12 acquisition months ending with the window's end month, refunds separate from churn *(Amended 2026-11-04 after phase 1, D-16)*; **churn by channel** = A-41 per channel; **cohort table** = customers, ARPA (offset-0 revenue ÷ customers), cohort blended CAC, refund rate per acquisition month. [`SOC_META_LAL2` Oct 2025 – Jan 2026 cohorts paid back at month 9; refund 2.1 %]
- A-49 **Rounding** only at display: `$188.4k`, `457`, `$531`, `10.8 mo`, `3.4×`, `14.2%`, `+7.0%`, `−1.9 pt`; Δ rows signed; pt for rates, % for amounts and counts.

## 6. The story (binding events; ADR-0015 (d))
