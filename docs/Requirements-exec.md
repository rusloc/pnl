## 2b. Period model — granularity picker (amended 2026-10-29, ADR-0013 (k); contract: `docs/ADD/features/exec-period-model.md`)

- E-90 **Granularity picker** replaces the period switcher: **Week · Month · Quarter · Year**, then **one item** of that granularity (single select, no date picker). Month = the 4-5-4 retail period (P10 FY2026 = W40–W43); Quarter = 13 weeks; Year = the 52-week FY (E-10). Default on open **Week 43 FY2026**: the first view equals the v5 story (ET-02 unchanged).
- E-91 **Items** are derived from `dim_week` in each flavour (no generator change): Week = every actual week FY2025 W1 … FY2026 W43 (95); Month = FY2025 P1 … FY2026 P10 (22); Quarter = FY2025 Q1 … FY2026 Q4 (8); Year = FY2025, FY2026 (2). Item keys `FY2026-W43` · `FY2026-P10` · `FY2026-Q4` · `FY2026`. Items without an actual week are not listed. Picker rows: granularity → fiscal year → item; changing the granularity keeps the item that contains the previous item's last week. *2026-10-30: at Year the JS hides the FY row and offers the two FY items as item chips; Power BI keeps the FY row, which drives the single Year chip — accepted platform difference (E-53, E-87).*
- E-92 **Window** of an item = its weeks ∩ actuals (≤ as-of). An item whose last week > as-of is **partial** and is named **"to date"** in the picker (`Q4 · to date`, `FY2026 · to date`), the header (`Quarter 4 to date · 28 Sep–25 Oct 2026`, `FY2026 to date · 29 Dec 2025–25 Oct 2026`) and the sub-titles (`quarter 4 to date`). At as-of W43 exactly Q4 FY2026 and FY2026 are partial; P10 FY2026 is complete and reads `Month 10` (round 3: "Month" is the only UI word for the 4-5-4 period; item chips stay `P1 … P12`; "period" survives only in column / key names).
- E-93 **Aggregation per granularity:** flow cards = ratio of sums over the window; point-in-time cards at the window's last week `L` (Weeks of cover, Availability); **Channel mix = shares over the whole window** (`Σ net sales per channel ÷ Σ net sales` over the item's weeks; plan = the same over Budget; USER 2026-10-29 — at Week this is the single week, so the v5 mix holds); Sell-through = season-to-date at `L`, N/A when `L` is outside a season; GMROI = R52W at `L`, N/A before FY2025 W52; LFL = key-joined window ÷ LY window; **EBITDA flash = month-to-date through `L` at Week (unchanged value, sub-title "month 10 to date") and the item's own window at Month / Quarter / Year** (bars: weekly / per month / per quarter / per year). **EBITDA at Week, comparator Previous (round 3, USER): the previous month cut to the same week count** — the picked week is week k of its month → weeks 1 … k of the previous month (P1 → the previous FY's P12); week 5 of a 5-week month has no like-length counterpart → Δ N/A (default). This replaces "month-to-date as of last week"; only EBITDA changes (every other card's Previous at Week stays `lw_week_key`). Full table per card: `exec-period-model.md` §4.
- E-94 **Previous item** comparator = the item before the picked one at the same granularity (previous week = `lw_week_key`; previous period; previous quarter; previous year). When the picked item is partial, the previous item is **cut to the same number of weeks from its start** (Q4 FY2026 to date → Q3 W27–W30; FY2026 to date → FY2025 W1–W43, which equals Last year). Labels: the comparator option is a **static chip "Previous"** in both flavours (as built; the long form `Previous week / month / quarter` appears only in the header comparator line — docs aligned 2026-10-30), Δ suffix **always short** `vs prev. wk / mth / qtr` (USER 2026-10-29; full Δ label set in E-98). **At Year "Previous" is not offered** (USER 2026-10-29): its window would equal Last year for FY2026 to date and be N/A for FY2025. A user on Previous who switches to Year **snaps to Last year**; the snap is not remembered when switching back down (default). In the URL `c=prev` (or legacy `lw`) with a Year item normalises to `c=ly` (E-62). Power BI: the Previous row is hidden from the comparator slicer at Year and the engine reads LY if it is still selected. **Last year** = the same weeks one year back by `ly_week_key` (partial Q4 → FY2025 W40–W43).
- E-95 **N/A cases (E-21):** Budget exists for FY2026 only → for FY2025 items every budget-based value, Δ and status is N/A (grey "–" chip, muted `n/a vs budget`); **target-based cards (Weeks of cover, Availability, Sell-through, GMROI, OTIF) keep their status in FY2025 where their input exists** — GMROI only from FY2025 W52 (R52W), Sell-through only in season — so an FY2025 item rates 3 or 4 cards, never 5 (corrected 2026-10-29); Last year is N/A for every FY2025 item (data start FY2025 W1); Previous item is N/A for the first item of the data. Counts and the dot run over the rated cards (N/A excluded); n = 0 → grey dot, tooltip "no rated card". No data-generation change.
- E-96 **Bars follow the granularity:** last **13 weeks · 12 months · 8 quarters · 2 years** ending at the picked item; each bar = the card's value for that item (a partial item shows its to-date value); the picked item's bar in the status colour; dotted target = the picked item's basis; a bar with no data or no history is blank (label kept in the ⓘ chart: `W31` · `P11` · `Q1 FY25` · `FY25`). Sizes per E-29.
- E-97 **Status bands** (E-22, `param_status_rule`) apply unchanged at every granularity — no band is week-specific; status always vs Budget / target (E-24).
- E-98 **Labels** *(round 3: "Month" is the only UI word — "Period" / "period" appear nowhere in the UI)*: header period line `Week 43 · 19–25 Oct 2026` (FY appended only when ≠ as-of FY: `Week 43 FY2025 · 20–26 Oct 2025`) · `Month 10 · 28 Sep–25 Oct 2026` · `Quarter 3 · 29 Jun–27 Sep 2026` · `FY2025 · 30 Dec 2024–28 Dec 2025`; title `Nordmark · Week 43 / Month 10 / Quarter 4 / FY2026`; sub-titles `month 10` / `month 10 to date`; comparator option chip `Previous` (static), header comparator line `vs Previous week / month / quarter`; window nouns `week / month / quarter / year` in ⓘ stats (`vs LY month`, `Month n to date`; the previous-item stat rows use the short forms `vs last week` (W, unchanged) / `vs prev. mth` / `vs prev. qtr` / `vs LY` at Year); the How-to-read "Bars" bullet names the four lengths. **Card-face Δ label set, both flavours (USER 2026-10-29, always short):** Budget basis as built — `vs budget` (Net sales, Total margin, Markdown, EBITDA, Front GM), `vs budget LFL` (LFL), `vs target` (Weeks of cover, Availability, Sell-through, GMROI, OTIF), `vs plan` (Channel mix); Last year `vs LY` (unchanged); Previous **`vs prev. wk` · `vs prev. mth` · `vs prev. qtr`** (replace `vs last week`; no Year form). ## 4. Metric definitions (binding for the generator and both flavours)

All amounts EUR, ex-VAT. "Window" = the selected period (E-13); "scope" = the selected region / format (E-15). Week-43 ALL values in brackets are the v5 story and the control-total anchors (ET-02).

- E-30 **Net sales** = Σ gross sales at ticket − Σ markdown value − Σ returns (`fact_sales_week`). [€61.4 m; Budget €62.9 m; Δ −€1.5 m / −2.4 %; Watch]
- E-31 **Like-for-like growth %** = Net sales of LFL stores in the window ÷ the same stores' Net sales in the LY window (E-12) − 1. LFL store = `open_date` ≥ 13 months before the window's first day, not closed, not refitted in the last 13 months; evaluated per period (monthly in the spec; per retail period here). Budget LFL from `fact_budget_week.lfl_growth_bud`. [+3.4 %; 131 of 142 stores; Budget 3.0 %; On track]
- E-32 **Front gross margin %** = (Net sales − Σ COGS at standard landed cost) ÷ Net sales. [30.1 %; COGS €42.9 m; front margin €18.5 m; Budget 30.0 %; On track]
- E-33 **Back margin €** = Σ `fact_sales_week.back_margin_alloc` over the window and scope. The generator accrues back margin per week × supplier (`fact_supplier_income_week`: `rebate_accrual` + `promo_funding` + `listing_fees`, contract-rate × qualifying purchases, provisional until the 10 Nov true-up) and allocates it to store × category by COGS share (residual to the largest cell), so every scope reconciles and Σ ALL equals Σ supplier accruals (ET-07). **Total margin %** = (front margin € + back margin €) ÷ Net sales. [back €3.8 m = 6.2 %; total 36.3 %; Budget 36.2 %; On track, prov.]
- E-34 **Markdown % of gross sales** = Σ markdown value ÷ Σ gross sales at ticket; markdown never enters the margin twice. [9.8 %; €6.7 m ÷ €68.4 m; Budget 8.5 %; Off track]
- E-35 **Weeks of cover (forward)** = (Σ on-hand cost + Σ on-order cost at the window's last week-end, scope's locations) ÷ forecast weekly COGS (`fact_forecast_week`, fallback: R4W average COGS when the forecast is blank). Target = cost-weighted average of `dim_category.cover_target_wks` = 9.5. [11.4 wks; €402 m + €88 m = €490 m ÷ €43.0 m; Watch]
- E-36 **Availability %** = Σ `in_stock_sku_days` ÷ Σ `ranged_sku_days` over the window's last week (top-500 sellers, sales-weighted counts pre-computed by the generator). [94.1 %; 467,700 ÷ 497,000; target 97 %; Off track]
- E-37 **Sell-through %** = units sold season-to-date ÷ (season opening stock units + receipts season-to-date), season per E-16. [63.1 %; target 62 %; On track]
- E-38 **GMROI** = front gross margin € over the trailing 52 weeks ÷ average on-hand cost over the 52 week-end snapshots (on-order and in-transit excluded) [OQ 11]. [2.7×; €1,016 m ÷ €376 m; target 2.5×; On track]
- E-39 **Supplier OTIF %** = Σ `po_lines_otif` ÷ Σ `po_lines_due` over the window. [95.4 %; target 95 %; On track]
