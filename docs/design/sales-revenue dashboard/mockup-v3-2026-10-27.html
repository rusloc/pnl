Loans issued by month vs Budget

What it shows
Loans disbursed per month (Actual), the target for the month (Budget) and the same month a year earlier (Prior year).
How to read it
Actual above Budget = ahead of plan. The yellow dot is the selected month.
Example
Sep 2026: 25.6k loans vs 26.2k Budget (−2.4 %) and 22.0k a year ago (+16.4 %).
Watch out
Open months under Actual are blank, never interpolated.
