Mirelo Sales & revenue - metric map, lineage and user flow

Companion to docs/ADD/features/sales-revenue-dashboard-concept.md (§9 addendum) and mockup.html. Re-ranks the KPI set around the USER's four priority metrics (2026-10-24), draws their lineage from the source tables, and fixes the reading path: start at the top, flow down to the explanation, finish at loan and client level.

DRAFT - projection state. Concept for USER approval; nothing built. No track code, PBIP, data or tests are affected by this page.

a. Metric map - three tiers

T1 the USER's four priorities - the top band of the Overview and the start of every reading path · T2 drivers that explain a Tier-1 movement ("why") - the band under each priority and the breakdown views · T3 outcomes and context (revenue, subscriptions, targets, risk cost) - still on the dashboard, never above a Tier-1 metric.

Tier-1 definitions (chosen, with the alternative stated)

1 · Loans issued - two numbers on one card: count and disbursed principal
Count = loans with disbursed_date in the window (one row of fact_loan each) · Principal = Σ principal_local ÷ rate of the loan's month and currency (actual rates; constant currency = budget rates of the selected year). Account openings are not loans and stay out. Comparators: Budget (target loans / principal) and Prior year. Direction ↑.
2 · New clients locked in
Chosen: clients whose first loan was disbursed in the window (dim_client.first_loan_date in window = Σ fact_loan.is_first_loan). "Locked in" means money moved: the client passed KYC, credit and take-up. Alternative (not recommended): first credit approval in the window - earlier signal, but 13 % of approvals are never taken up, so it over-counts and does not reconcile to loans. A second alternative, first account opened, is an account metric and stays Tier 3 (New accounts). Comparators: Budget (new-client target), Prior year. Direction ↑.
3 · Returning clients %
Two denominators, both kept, one on top: Chosen headline (client-weighted): distinct borrowers in the window with ≥ 1 prior repaid or current loan ÷ distinct borrowers in the window (52.4 % YTD). This is literally "share of clients who took a loan again". Sub-line (loan-weighted): loans to returning clients ÷ loans issued (57.8 % - the concept's "repeat share"); higher because returning clients take more loans per period. A prior loan that was written off does not count as "returning" (the client would not be re-lent anyway; it would flatter the ratio). The cohort view (Tier 2) answers the time question: of clients whose first loan was in month m, the share with a second loan within 90 / 180 days. Comparators: Prior year (pp); no budget. Direction ↑.
4 · Bad / overdue loans %
Chosen headline: 30+ days-past-due ratio by balance = balance of loans ≥ 30 DPD ÷ outstanding principal, at the last closed month of the window (4.6 %). Portfolio standard, comparable across tenors, insensitive to ticket size. Sub-lines: 90+ DPD ratio (2.1 %, "bad"), 30+ DPD by count of active loans (5.3 %, "overdue"), and the vintage bad rate = loans ever 90+ DPD within 6 months of disbursement ÷ loans of the vintage (3.4 %) - the number that ties credit quality back to the sales period in which the loans were issued. Comparators: Budget (planned risk appetite) and Prior year, pp. Direction ↓ (down = favourable, arrow still shows direction).

c. Visual / semantic flow - start at the top, flow down, finish at loan level

Reading order is the layout: START is the top band of the Overview (four hero cards: value, Δ vs Budget and Prior year, 13-month trend). NEXT is the explanation band directly under each card - the breakdown that answers "why did it move". FINISH is the loan-level and client-level detail table, one drill away from any breakdown mark, with every filter carried over. Progressive disclosure: nothing below a hero card is needed to read the hero card; nothing in the detail table is needed to read the breakdown.

Hidden drill-through pages loan_detail and client_detail (table visuals, ≤ 500 rows shown, "Export data" for more), drill-through fields = the carried filters; native Back button; "Show loans" as a drill-through button on each breakdown card; row click → drill-through again (loan → client). Track A (Rayfin)

In-window detail panel sliding in from the right (720 px, over the content; rail, title row and tabs stay visible), virtualised table, column sort, "Export CSV", Escape / ✕ closes; filters carried as chips at the top of the panel. Loan rows come from one paged DAX query against the semantic model (filters → query, page size 200).

Track C (Next.js)

Same panel as A; loan-level Parquet is not in the first load: one file per disbursement month under public/data/sales/loans/, fetched on first drill for the months in the window (≈ 0.4 MB each), cached for the session; "Copy link" encodes the drill state (F-37 analogue).

Page level - how the four flows share the Overview and where each leaves

Overview = four hero cards (START) over a 2 × 2 "why" grid, one mini-explanation per Tier-1 metric, each with an "Open …" exit to its view. Revenue & targets (Tier 3) is reached from the tabs row, not from a hero card. Every view reaches the same Loan detail; Clients reaches Client detail first. The tabs row keeps all five views one click away at any depth; the applied-filters pop-up and the rail work at every level.
