Status rules (status is always vs target, independent of the comparator)

MetricRule

Where the margins meet

Front margin = Net sales − COGS at landed cost (POS lines). Back margin = contract rate × qualifying purchases, accrued weekly from the supplier-income fact and provisional until the monthly true-up. Total margin € = front + back; Total margin % divides it by Net sales. Markdown already reduces the sell price, so it never enters the margin sum twice: it is shown as markdown % of gross sales and as a step in the margin bridge. EBITDA = Total margin − shrink − store OpEx − central OpEx − fulfilment.

Data contract (five facts, three keys)

  • Facts: Sales (day × store/channel × SKU), Inventory snapshot (day × location × SKU, semi-additive), Purchases / receipts (PO line × receipt), Supplier income (contract × period), GL / OpEx (account × cost centre × period × scenario). Traffic & web optional.
  • LY week key on the retail calendar: every LY comparison joins week 43 to LY week 43, never to the same dates; the 53-week year is restated per policy.
  • LFL flag on the store, recalculated monthly (open ≥ 13 months, not refitted); LFL filters both current and LY sales to the same store set.
  • Last snapshot for stock (MAX snapshot date in the window); provisional flag on back margin and EBITDA flash until close.
  • Executive model stays day × store × category (≤ 500 MB); SKU grain only behind a click; receipt, PO and GL lines in the domain models.

Open questions for the USER (each with a default)

  1. Retail calendar · 4-5-4 or calendar months, week start, 53-week handling? Default: 4-5-4, weeks Mon–Sun, FY from the Monday nearest 1 Feb; LY always by week key.
  2. Countries and currency · one or several, which reporting currency? Default: NL + BE, EUR only, no FX layer.
  3. Private label · split on the landing page or only on drill pages? Default: drill pages only; bench KPI "Private-label share %".
  4. Back-margin method · weekly contract-rate accrual with monthly true-up, or finance-posted amounts only? Default: accrual, shown provisional until true-up.
  5. THE number · Net sales, Total margin % or Weeks of cover as the page's headline? Default: Net sales first; a profit-first page swaps Total margin € into the lead.
  6. Availability definition · all ranged SKUs or top sellers, weighted? Default: top-500 by LY sales, sales-weighted.
  7. Channels and formats in scope · marketplace? three formats? Default: Store (hyper, super, convenience), Online, Wholesale; marketplace on the bench.
  8. Landed cost · standard cost with PPV, or actual incl. freight and duty? Default: standard landed cost on the POS line; PPV on the Supplier page.

Build risks (tri-track, top three)

  • Retail calendar · every LY figure, LFL and "same week" claim depends on the LY week key and the 53-week policy.
  • Grain and size · 3 years × 142 stores × 68k SKUs × days is far beyond an executive model; landing page on a day × store × category aggregation, SKU grain only behind a click.
  • Card visual limits (Track B) · column sparklines, chip tints and the pop-ups become SVG measures, fx shapes and tooltip pages; each adds a query, so the N-01 budget must be measured on the first build.
