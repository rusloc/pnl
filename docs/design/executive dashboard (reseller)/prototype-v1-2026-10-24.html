a

Metric map - 32 KPIs, three tiers, one page that hangs on three numbers

A reseller makes money on three levers: sell more at the right price, keep more of each sale, turn stock faster than it costs to hold. The page hangs on one number per lever. Everything else on the landing page is an explanation of those three; everything on the drill pages is an explanation of the explanations.

Tier 1 - the three numbers the page hangs on
  1. Net sales (THE number) - the top line every other metric divides by or adds up to; it is what the CEO is asked about first on Monday, and LFL, mix, pace and the category movers all exist to explain it.
  2. Total margin % (front + back) - "keep more of each sale" in one figure. Front margin alone hides the supplier-funded half of a reseller's profit; back margin alone is provisional. Together they are the margin the business actually keeps, and markdown, shrink and category mix explain its movement.
  3. Weeks of cover (forward) - the operational stock-health number: it decides this week's actions (stop buying, bring intake forward, mark down). Availability (too little stock) and markdown (too much) are its two failure modes; sell-through and GMROI are its slower, financial readings. GMROI was the alternative; it is R12M and moves slowly, so it serves better as a Tier-2 explanation. See open question 5.

Tier 2 = the 12 landing tiles (★) that explain Tier 1, grouped in three bands: Margin, Stock, Supply & profit, plus the two "where" visuals (channel mix, top category movers). Tier 3 = the bench, shipped inactive in the kpi table and switched on per client. Comparators: B budget · LY last year same retail week (LY week key, not date) · LW last week · F forecast. Direction: ↑ higher is better, ↓ lower is better, ◦ context (band around target).

Metric lineage - from five facts to the three Tier-1 numbers

Left to right: facts and dimensions → base measures (additive sums, last-snapshot values, accruals) → intermediate money and stock amounts → ratios (the Tier-2 tiles) → the Tier-1 numbers. Edge labels name the operation. Hover a node for its definition; cobalt edges are the Tier-1 paths.

factdimension (filter / key)base measureintermediate amountTier-2 ratioTier-1labels: Σ sum · ÷ ratio · − / + arithmetic · last snap · LY key · accrual · fcst
Where the margins meet. Front margin = Net sales − COGS at landed cost (POS lines). Back margin = contract rate × qualifying purchases, accrued weekly from the supplier-income fact and flagged provisional until the monthly true-up. Total margin € = front + back; Total margin % divides it by Net sales. Markdown is already inside Net sales (it reduces the sell price), so it never enters the margin sum twice: it is shown as markdown % of gross sales and as a step in the margin bridge. Shrink is a cost below total margin: EBITDA = Total margin − shrink − store OpEx − central OpEx − fulfilment, then ÷ Net sales for the margin %.
Two keys that decide whether the numbers are right. (1) LY week key on the retail calendar - every LY comparison joins week 43 to LY week 43, never to the same dates; the 53-week year is restated per policy (open question 1). (2) LFL flag on the store - recalculated monthly (open ≥ 13 months, not refitted); LFL growth filters both current and LY sales to the same store set, so new stores, closures and refits sit in the non-LFL bridge step, not in the ratio.

Design direction - "Trading sheet": a Monday-meeting page, not a finance ledger

The P&L is read like a ledger: cool, white, navy rail, condensed statement type. A trading dashboard is read like the Monday trading sheet on a retailer's wall: a dark header strip with the week and the refresh time, warm paper underneath, big numbers in a sturdy grotesk, weekly columns rather than smooth lines, status as a coloured rail under each tile. Nothing from the Ledger token set is reused: new type family, new ground, new accent, new status set, new tile anatomy, new grid.

SUBJECT Monday trading dashboard for a reseller's leadership (CEO, CFO, Commercial, Supply chain, Retail ops, E-com); purpose inform + decide; lifespan system (3 tracks) STYLE G1 Swiss / International + modifier trading sheet (dark ticker band, warm paper body, weekly columns) · mood brisk, warm, matter-of-fact · build HTML/CSS (A, C), PBIR theme + native visuals (B) PALETTE ground #EFECE4 tile #FCFBF8 ink #1E1A17 muted #6B645C accent #2048C6 band #1E1A17 · status good #136C3A / watch #9A5B00 / off #C0272D · checks PASS (fills #2E9E5B #E3A52A #D9534F FILL-ONLY) TYPE display Archivo 700 (tnum, wdth 100) · text Archivo 400/500 · tables Archivo wdth 85 · scale 11/12/13/15/18/24/30/40 · fallback Segoe UI / Segoe UI Semibold (Bahnschrift optional) · subsets latin CANVAS 1440×810 · grid 8 px / 12 cols × 100 + 16 gutter / 32 margin · stroke 1 px rules, 3 px status rail · composition band → ticker → hero → three explanation bands → footer; focal point = net sales value

Why this direction

  • Retail reads in weeks. Thirteen weekly columns on every tile and in the hero carry the trading rhythm (promo weeks, events) better than a smoothed line; the current week is solid, the rest stone grey, the budget a dotted line.
  • The band is the meeting agenda. One charcoal strip holds the week, the refresh stamp and the three controls, so a screenshot always says which week and which comparator it was. The ticker underneath states the agenda: counts and the off-track sentence.
  • Warm paper, not cool grey. Stone ground and off-white tiles separate it from the Ledger at a glance, project well, and keep the three status hues vivid without saturating the page.
  • Archivo is an open-licence grotesk with tabular figures and a width axis: wide and heavy for the hero value, narrow for the dense drill tables, one family for everything. In Power BI it falls back to Segoe UI / Segoe UI Semibold at the same sizes; Bahnschrift (installed on Windows 10+) is an optional closer match if the USER accepts a Windows-only fallback.
  • Status is a rail, a chip and a word. Each tile ends in a 3 px rail in the status fill, carries a square chip with glyph + word top-right, and colours only the Δ line - never colour alone, and one hue per tile.

Alternatives considered (one line each)

  • "Departures board" - full charcoal page, amber tabular numerals in a mono (JetBrains Mono), for a screen on the trading-room wall; strongest character, weakest in Power BI and print.
  • "Retail broadsheet" - editorial: Fraunces / Newsreader headlines, paper ground, hairlines and small caps; calm and board-pack friendly, less "Monday 8 a.m.".
  • "Fluent native" - Power BI defaults (Segoe, white, Fluent status greens / reds); fastest in Track B, least distinct from every other Power BI report.

Open questions for the USER (each with a default)

  1. Retail calendar - 4-5-4 or calendar months, week start, 53-week handling? Default: 4-5-4 with weeks Mon–Sun, FY starting on the Monday nearest 1 Feb (week 43 = 19–25 Oct 2026 as in the frame), 53rd week every 5–6 years restated against LY week 1 per Nordmark's policy; LY always by week key.
  2. Countries and currency - one country or several, and which reporting currency? Default: Netherlands + Belgium, EUR only, no FX layer on this report (keeps the model and the parity tests simple; a multi-currency version reuses the P&L's translation rules later).
  3. Private label - split private label from branded on the landing page or only on the drill pages? Default: PL flag on the product dimension; PL share of sales and PL margin on the Margin and Sales pages; one bench KPI "Private-label share %" switched off on the landing page.
  4. Back-margin method - contract-rate accrual weekly with a monthly true-up, or finance-posted amounts only? Default: accrual = contract rate × qualifying purchases, weekly, shown with the PROV. badge; the monthly true-up replaces the accrual and clears the badge; earned vs invoiced on the Supplier page.
  5. THE number - which Tier-1 is the one the page is named after: Net sales, Total margin % or Weeks of cover (or GMROI)? Default: Net sales in the hero; Total margin % and Weeks of cover lead their bands; GMROI stays Tier 2. If the USER prefers a profit-first page, Total margin € becomes the hero and Net sales moves to the Margin band.
  6. Availability definition - all ranged SKUs or the top sellers, weighted? Default: top-500 SKUs by LY sales, weighted by sales, measured as SKU-store-days in stock ÷ ranged; all-SKU availability on the Inventory page.
  7. Channels and formats in scope - store, online, wholesale; marketplace? three formats? Default: Store (hyper, super, convenience), Online, Wholesale; marketplace off (bench); region = NL / BE.
  8. Landed cost - standard cost with PPV, or actual landed cost including freight and duty? Default: standard landed cost on the POS line (front margin stable week to week) plus the purchase price variance KPI on the Supplier page; actual cost at month-end close.

Not asked (decided by the spec): three header slicers only; status vs target independent of the comparator; 12 landing tiles; drill pages hidden from navigation; daily 05:00 refresh.
