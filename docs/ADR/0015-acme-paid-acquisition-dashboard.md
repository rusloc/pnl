## 1. Context

The spec describes a one-page, five-view analytical dashboard for the paid online campaigns of a SaaS product sold on web, iOS and Android: six channels (paid search, paid social, app install, display and video, retargeting, affiliates), 17 campaigns in the mockup, four metric tiers (vitals → funnel → efficiency and mix → diagnostics), guardrails beside the vitals, two framings of cost (platform-reported vs modelled), a pop-up filter window with chips instead of a filter pane, a matte-glass visual system with a fixed six-slot channel palette, and a star schema of six facts. Its "why chain" is the story the data must tell: spend up and CAC up → paid-to-trial fell, not click volume → it fell on the 18th → only on mobile, only on paid social → one campaign carries 70 % of the loss → its top creative's CTR halved at frequency 3.4 → store page CVR flat, so the leak is pre-click → prior cohorts from that campaign still pay back in month 9 → attribution gap normal, data 2 h old. Decision in the story: rotate creative, hold budget.

Two things the precedents settled and this ADR keeps: a **deterministic, story-first Python generator writing local Parquet** is the data home of a showcase (ADR-0013 (b)–(c)), and the **Power BI flavour is its own PBIP importing that folder through `DataFolder`** (ADR-0011 (b), ADR-0013 (g)). Two things differ from Nordmark: the USER's words point at the **Power BI flavour here and the data + generator for "another agent"**, and the spec's own mockup is **arithmetically inconsistent in several places** (CPM, platform funnel rates, blended-CAC delta, channel pacing vs plan, brand share, campaign spend sum), so the headline numbers have to be re-derived as a consistent parameter set before the generator can hit them by construction. Decision (d) does that; the mockup values are kept wherever they are consistent.

### (b) Data home: `data/acme_marketing_dummy_data/`, deterministic Python generator, local Parquet, no lakehouse

**Default:** `data/acme_marketing_dummy_data/` in the Nordmark layout — `generator/generate.py` + `generator/requirements.txt` (numpy 2.5.4, pandas 2.3.3, pyarrow 26.0.0, as Mirelo / Nordmark; venv gitignored), `parquet/` (one snappy file per table + `manifest.json`), `checks/control_totals_usd.csv`, `checks/verify.py` (independent recomputation, exit 1 on any failure, byte-identical rerun), `README.md` (tables, columns, relationships, measure rules — **the data contract both flavours build against**; table and column names there win over any spec or mockup name, ADR-0013 (j)). **Seed 20261104**, `--out <folder>`, `verify.py --data <folder>`. **No CSV copies** (Nordmark open point 1 became the norm: Parquet only; the `dims/` / `facts/` / `params/` CSV folders of the Mirelo layout are not written). Types as Nordmark: `date32` dates, `timestamp[ms]` for sync stamps *(Amended 2026-11-04 after phase 1: was `timestamp[s]`; Parquet has no seconds unit, the values are whole minutes — D-8)*, `bool`, `int32` / `int64`, `double` for every amount and ratio (never DECIMAL), `string` codes. Amounts USD, rounded to the cent.

**Why:** ADR-0013 (b): portability is the governing constraint of a showcase that another repo hosts; a lakehouse schema would make the pack depend on a tenant and on a load + refresh cycle before the first pixel. **Later (not this build):** schema `acme_marketing` in `lh_raw` via a `fabric-data/lh_raw/load_acme_marketing.py` in the `load_acme_pl.py` pattern; the PBI model then switches partition sources from `DataFolder` to the `SqlEndpoint` / `Database` parameters only.

**Alternatives:** lakehouse first (rejected, above); CSV into Power Query (Parquet is typed and smaller; the Trucks / Mirelo / Nordmark PBIPs prove the path); Parquet + CSV both (Mirelo layout) — no consumer reads the CSVs any more.

### (c) The data contract: tables, grain, columns, volumes — reduced to what the dashboard reads

The spec's star schema (`fact_ad_delivery` at day × ad × platform × placement, `fact_install` per install, `fact_product_event` per user × event, `fact_billing` per subscription × month, `fact_pipeline` per opportunity, `bridge_attribution` per conversion × model) is a warehouse design. No card reads below the grains below, so the generator writes these instead. **This section gates the generator; the data README restates it with every column type.**

**Calendar and as-of.** Daily grain **2025-01-01 … 2026-11-03** (672 actual days); `dim_date` runs to 2026-12-31 (730 rows) so the Nov / Dec 2026 budget exists for pacing. As-of **2026-11-04 14:55** (`manifest.json › refreshed`); last complete day **2026-11-03** (`as_of_date`). Default window **R30 = Oct 5 – Nov 3 2026**; previous period Sep 5 – Oct 4 2026; same period last year Oct 5 – Nov 3 2025 — all inside the data. Comparisons join on calendar dates (`dim_date.ly_date` = the same date one year back); there is no retail calendar here.

**Scope attributes live on the campaign.** Each campaign targets **one platform** (Web / iOS / Android), **one channel**, **one network**, one market and one tier. The Platform, Channel, Network, Market and Tier filters are therefore campaign filters and no fact carries those codes; the platform funnel, the channel matrix and the platform-reported gap all group through `dim_campaign`. *Alternative:* `platform_code` and `channel_code` on every fact (a strict star, two more columns per fact, verify ties them) — pick it if a campaign is ever to span platforms; reversal cost low.

**Grain reductions and what they cost.** Per-ad rows → ad set × creative (the dashboard never names an ad; the creative is the fatigue unit). Per-install and per-user events → daily stage counts per campaign (+ the segment and term splits), cohorted by sign-up day (a window sums days; a day's activations are "within 7 days of that sign-up day", so the last 7 days of a window are partial by construction — the ⓘ says so; the mockup's own 7-day rate has the same property). Subscription × month billing → acquisition-month cohort × campaign × offset (payback, retention of revenue, refunds, churn all read from it; nothing needs the individual subscription). Opportunities → month × campaign (no pipeline card in v1). Touch-level attribution bridge → **precomputed credits per model** in `fact_attribution_day`: the four models re-split the same first-party conversions (Σ over campaigns per day is identical for DDA / LAST_CLICK / FIRST_TOUCH); PLATFORM is the networks' over-claim. The **model switch applies to paid customers and New MRR only** (and so to CAC, CPA, ROAS, payback, LTV:CAC); sign-ups, activations and trials are product-analytics counts and do not switch. Cohort facts are written under DDA only. The spec's **lookback and conversion-event selectors are not built** (one lookback per model; one conversion event — paid); see §5.

**Relationships in the model:** every fact `date` → `dim_date`; `month_key` / `cohort_month` → `dim_month`; `campaign_key` → `dim_campaign`; `dim_campaign.channel_code` → `dim_channel`, `.network_code` → `dim_network`, `.platform_code` → `dim_platform` (**one-level outriggers through the campaign** — allowed by this ADR as the exception to the Track B "no snowflaking without an ADR" rule; filter direction single, toward the facts); `ad_set_key` → `dim_ad_set`, `creative_key` → `dim_creative`, `term_key` → `dim_term`, `page_key` → `dim_landing_page`, `segment_code` → `dim_platform_segment`, `model_code` → `dim_attribution_model`. `dim_ad_set.campaign_key`, `dim_creative.campaign_key`, `dim_term.campaign_key` and `dim_platform_segment.platform_code` are label columns without relationships (they would create second paths). `param_*`, `sync_log` and `dim_onboarding_step` join no fact.

**Market and tier are campaign attributes** (GLOBAL campaigns appear under no single market; a Market pick lists the campaigns targeted at it). The spec's Audience group (new vs returning, device, country) has no data behind it in this build (§5).

### (d) The story is a parameter set; the generator hits it by construction

**Default:** story-first allocation as Nordmark (ADR-0013 (c)): the ALL-scope daily totals of every card input (impressions, clicks, spend, sessions, store views, installs, sign-ups, steps, trials, paid credits per model, new MRR, plan) are fixed from story series for the default window, its previous period and the whole span (seasonality: weekday dips, summer trough, Q4 build, spend growing from ≈ $110 k a month in Jan 2025 to ≈ $190 k per 30 days in Oct 2026), then allocated to campaign × ad set × creative (× term, × page, × segment) cells by stable shares plus seeded noise, each day's rounding residual to the largest cell. The window figures below are then exact at display precision, not tuned.

*Amended 2026-11-04 after phase 1:* the data README's **"Deviations from ADR-0015" (D-1 … D-18)** in `data/acme_marketing_dummy_data/README.md` is **authoritative for the generator**; where it and this section differ, the README wins and the line here carries a dated note. Phase 1 as built: 27 tables, 257,188 rows, 2.19 MB, `control_totals_usd.csv` 28,660 rows, `verify.py` 132 / 0 with a byte-identical rerun; every binding value below holds except where a note says otherwise. - **Risks:** (1) the story is dense — 30 days, one campaign, one creative, two alerts, cohort payback 10 months back; a generator that hits every binding value may produce implausible neighbours (e.g. other campaigns' daily noise) — `verify.py` asserts plausibility bands as Nordmark's `generate.py` does. (2) **Gross margin 36.3 %** is low for SaaS and visible in the ⓘ; if the USER picks 75 %, payback becomes ≈ 5.2 months and LTV:CAC ≈ 7.0× and the "worsening but inside" story is gone — decide before phase 2 (§5 q 3). (3) Fractional DDA credits per campaign read oddly in a table ("30.9 paid customers") — the campaign table rounds to integers, the ⓘ shows the credit; the JS flavour must do the same. (4) 1440 × 810 holds three card rows of ≈ 182 px; tables scroll inside cards and the creatives grid becomes a table — accepted; the JS flavour may grow taller (Mirelo precedent). (5) The reported-vs-modelled gap and the model switch are precomputed; a reader expecting the lookback selector to change numbers will find it absent (footer and ⓘ say so). (6) Scale: ≈ $190 k per 30 days is six figures, inside the spec's "six to low seven figures" but below the main agent's 0.6–1.2 M gloss; a scale change after phase 2 means regenerating and re-testing every control total (cheap in the generator, a day in the tests).
